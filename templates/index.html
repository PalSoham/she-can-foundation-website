ESTABLISHED FOR SOCIAL CHANGE

Empowering Underprivileged Women and Girls to Shape the Future.

The She Can Foundation is a registered non-governmental organization in India working tirelessly to create a more equitable society. Through education, hygiene awareness, skill development, and active advocacy, we dismantle the socio-economic barriers that limit women from achieving their highest potential.

WHO WE ARE

A Vision of Equitable Opportunities

We believe in a world where every woman has the resources, skills, and support system to thrive. By focusing on practical interventions, we empower women to become self-reliant, breaking cycles of poverty and gender inequality in their communities.

A Mission of Collaborative Action

Our programs are designed in collaboration with local communities, ensuring sustainable impact. From distributing hygiene kits to providing job training, we believe that empowering a woman transforms entire generations.

02 /

OUR PILLARS OF IMPACT

I. Menstrual Hygiene & Health

Breaking taboos by facilitating educational workshops and distributing sanitary napkins to young girls in rural schools and marginalized urban areas.

II. Education & Skill Training

Offering computer literacy, vocational courses, and financial training programs designed to secure economic independence for underprivileged women.

III. Advocacy & Leadership

Conducting awareness campaigns on women's legal rights, domestic violence prevention, and leadership mentorship for rising young leaders.
